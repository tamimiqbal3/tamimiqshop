I am Tamim Iqbal — a student currently pursuing my Higher Secondary Certificate at New Government Degree College, Rajshahi, where I am also an active member of the NGDC Science Club.

My work sits at the intersection of design, development, and leadership. I build digital systems — attendance platforms, event registration tools, and community portals — that solve real problems for real people.

Beyond the screen, I am deeply involved in community organizing. From coordinating science festivals to managing debate programs, I approach every initiative with the same precision I bring to code.

I am particularly interested in artificial intelligence and how it can be applied responsibly in educational and community contexts. My long-term goal is to contribute to the technology ecosystem of Bangladesh.
